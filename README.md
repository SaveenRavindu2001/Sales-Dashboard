# Sales-Dashboard
Interactive Excel Sales Dashboard designed to analyze sales performance using KPIs, Pivot Tables, Charts, and Slicers. The dashboard provides insights into sales trends, regional performance, product categories, sales channels, customer segments, and salesperson performance.

## Project Objectives
- To develop an interactive Sales Dashboard using Microsoft Excel.
- To analyze sales performance across different regions, product categories, sales channels, and customer segments.
- To track key performance indicators (KPIs) such as Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Orders, Quantity Sold, Average Order Value, and Profit Margin.
- To identify monthly sales trends and understand changes in sales performance over time.
- To evaluate salesperson and sales channel performance.
- To provide interactive data filtering using slicers for easier business analysis.
- To present sales data in a clear and visually appealing dashboard to support data-driven decision-making.

## Questions (KPI's)
- What is the total sales revenue?
- What is the total profit generated?
- How many orders were placed?
- What is the total quantity of products sold?
- What is the average order value?
- What is the overall profit margin percentage?
- Which region generates the highest sales?
- Which product category contributes the most to sales?
- Which salesperson has the highest sales performance?
- Which sales channel generates the highest sales?
- How do sales trends change over time?
- Which customer segment contributes the most to total sales?

## Process

- Collected and prepared the sales dataset containing 1,200 records.
- Cleaned and organized the data for analysis.
- Created Pivot Tables to summarize sales, profit, orders, quantity, and performance.
- Developed KPI calculations for key business metrics.
- Created Pivot Charts to visualize sales trends and performance.
- Added interactive Slicers for filtering the dashboard.
- Designed and formatted the final Sales Dashboard in Microsoft Excel.
- Analyzed the dashboard to identify sales trends and business performance.
